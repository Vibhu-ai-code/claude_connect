# Privacy Policy - Moolchand Backlink Builder (Chrome extension)

_Last updated: 19 August 2026_

Publish this page at a public URL and give that URL to the Chrome Web Store.
Replace `[contact email]` with a mailbox you monitor, for example

## Summary

The extension has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything it stores
stays inside your own browser. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any
personal data.

## What the extension stores

Two things, both in Chrome's local extension storage on your own device:

1. **Your settings** — brand name, website URL, target domain, business address,
   phone number, the sender name, role and email used in outreach drafts, and
   your UTM tag preferences. You type these in yourself on the Settings page.
2. **Your prospect list** — the URLs, names, categories, statuses, notes and
   link-check results for the sites you choose to track.

This data never leaves the browser. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and
we have no ability to read it. Removing the extension deletes it.

## What the extension reads

When you open the popup, click Re-scan, or use a right-click menu item, the
extension reads the page you are currently viewing to count its links, read
anchor text and `rel` attributes, detect mentions of your own domain, and pick
up a visible editorial contact address if the page shows one. This happens only
on that explicit action, only on the tab you are looking at, and the page
content is used in memory to draw the result on screen. It is not stored,
logged or transmitted, and the page itself is never modified.

When you press **Verify** on a tracked prospect, the extension requests that URL
directly from the site in question and checks the returned HTML for links to
your domain. That request goes to the site itself, exactly as if you had opened
the page in a tab; only the resulting one-line summary is saved to your local
prospect list.

## What the extension does not do

- No data is sent to any server operated by us — there is none.
- No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, advertising or fingerprinting.
- No remote code: all code ships inside the package.
- No browsing history collection. The extension only ever looks at a page when
  you act on that page.
- No automated posting, commenting or form submission on any website.

## Permissions and why they exist

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Keep your settings and prospect list on your device. |
| `activeTab`, `scripting` | Read the current page's links when you ask for a scan. |
| `tabs` | Label a scan or saved prospect with the page URL and title, and open sources you click in a new tab. |
| `contextMenus` | Provide the right-click "save as prospect" shortcuts. |
| Access to all websites | Backlinks can be on any site, so you must be able to scan any page you open and verify any prospect you added. |
